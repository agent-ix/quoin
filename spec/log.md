* **2026-08-19** — **CR-018**: new [FR-039](./functional/FR-039-mutation-score-threshold.md) — **mutation score as a declared verification threshold** (agent-ix/quoin#48). * **2026-08-17** — **FR-032** (new FR): the **evidence auditor** — suspect links, staleness, vacuity (agent-ix/quoin#80, epic agent-ix/quire-rs#81 P1, the last ticket of the track). A trace link was a string match that never expired, and evidence rotted three ways with none of them detected: the statement changed after binding (the DOORS suspect-link idea, stolen deliberately); the binding named a missing run or one behind HEAD; every tagged symbol was skipped or never reported. **The auditor runs nothing** (ADR-0011 invariant 1) — it reads the store and reports, and that separation is what makes the report trustworthy, because an auditor that could re-run a suite could also make a finding disappear by re-running it. Four judgement calls are recorded. **Severity says what kind of wrong**: high is evidence that *claims to exist and does not hold*; medium is ordinary work in progress — an unwritten test and a lie about a written one are different problems, and grading them the same teaches readers to skim both. **Independence is measured in suites**, not symbols: two symbols in one suite share a harness, fixtures and a failure mode, so counting them as two methods would let one broken assumption look like corroboration. **A check that cannot be performed is skipped, never guessed**: without a catalog, method conformance is not asked, because an absent catalog means the question cannot be asked rather than that the answer is yes. And the auditor **stops at the first disqualifying finding per obligation**, because reporting four consequences of one cause is how a report becomes unreadable. Every check is a pure function of its inputs — no clock, no filesystem walk, no subprocess inside the audit — which is what makes the whole thing testable without a repository. **Verified end-to-end**: record → bind → reword the criterion → `[high] suspect-link` → affirm → healthy. Matrix: TC-137..TC-144. Closes agent-ix/quoin#80.
