# Quoin assurance and record-producing feature rating

## Rationale

This is a proposed supplier rating for the stated intended use, not an accepted
decision. A failure in these features could alter which requirements, source
artifacts, results, or approvals are represented, or could cause incomplete
evidence to appear complete. Reviewers use these records and reports to assess
verification and traceability, so a misleading result could affect that
assessment. The rating calls for focused checks of the declared behavior,
including invalid and boundary inputs, exact persisted data, and refusal paths.

## Assurance activities

| Activity | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Exercise command parsing and module resolution with valid, missing, and conflicting inputs; inspect the selected command, contract, and output. | FR-001-AC-1; FR-006-AC-1; FR-009-AC-1; FR-012-AC-1; FR-015-AC-1; FR-070-AC-1; FR-073-AC-1 |
| Run evidence-store and change-assurance checks with repeated, changed, and invalid inputs; compare retained bytes and refusal status. | FR-030-AC-1; FR-030-AC-2; FR-048-AC-1; FR-063-AC-1; FR-064-AC-1; FR-065-AC-1; FR-098-AC-2; FR-100-AC-2 |
| Exercise traceability, audit, and measurement calculations against small enumerated fixtures and independently compare the resulting statuses and rates. | FR-032-AC-1; FR-035-AC-1; FR-037-AC-1; FR-086-AC-1; FR-090-AC-1; FR-107-AC-1; FR-115-AC-1; FR-131-AC-1; FR-153-AC-1 |
| Verify discharge and approval outcomes with complete and incomplete evidence and with accepted and rejected signer inputs. | FR-046-AC-1; FR-093-AC-1; FR-188-AC-1; FR-201-AC-1; FR-208-AC-1; FR-211-AC-1; FR-225-AC-1 |
| Compare generated dossier and assurance exports with their source records, including missing-source and malformed-model cases. | FR-040-AC-1; FR-191-AC-1; FR-192-AC-1; FR-194-AC-1; FR-196-AC-1; FR-222-AC-1; FR-226-AC-1; FR-234-AC-1 |

| Exercise independent kit verification with complete and incomplete runs, identity conflicts, and seeded mismatches; inspect the overall outcome and findings. | FR-237-AC-1; FR-237-AC-2; FR-237-AC-3; FR-237-AC-4; FR-237-AC-5; FR-237-AC-6 |
