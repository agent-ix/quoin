# SR-20261113052925: Failure-domain review of licence and ban policy findings (FR-150; FR-142, FR-144 amendments)

## Summary

Ticket: PLAT-1671. Unstated failure modes in the licence-policy path: coverage of the record set, refused and stale recordings, disposition scope, SPDX input and CLI misuse. Covered well: no licence is unknown, never allowed (FR-150-AC-2); a refused policy writes nothing (FR-150-AC-7); a vacuous SBOM shows nothing clean (FR-142-AC-33); a no-purl finding is unmatched, not dropped (FR-142-AC-32).

## Verdict

FAIL on one high and three medium findings, with two low findings.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Summary | Refs |
|----|----------|---------|------|
| FND-001 | high | FR-142 says licence-policy 'assesses every purl-bearing component of the SBOM it was run over', but the free-of rule only checks that the newest scan was read, is not vacuous and attached nothing to the record. The scan records only a component count and policy entry counts (FR-150), not which purls it evaluated, so the join cannot tell a record the scan evaluated from one it never saw. Concrete case: licence-policy runs over Monday's SBOM; Tuesday a new dependency is added, the SBOM regenerated and promoted (FR-141), and licence-policy not re-run. The new record is shown `no policy findings in the scan read` though no policy ever looked at it, which is the clean-versus-unrun confusion FR-034 exists to prevent. The same holds for a repository with more than one SBOM, since the join reads one scan per tool. Fix: record the evaluated purls in the scan (for example as the ruleset or a component list), state a record absent from it `policy findings not assessed: not in the scan read`, and add an AC with a record added after the scan. | spec/functional/FR-142-advisory-join-onto-soup-records.md:59-63; spec/functional/FR-150-licence-and-ban-policy-findings.md:80-81 |
| FND-002 | medium | FR-142 says 'If a licence-policy policy or SBOM was refused at recording, then the join SHALL report the tool as not run', and AC-30 'A policy or SBOM refused at recording leaves it not run'. The join observes only retained records and reads the newest one, so with an earlier licence-policy scan retained a refused recording leaves that older scan read (as FR-142-AC-22 states for grype). The likely real case is a person tightening the policy, mistyping it, and the join continuing to show the older, more permissive result. Fix: state it as grype does: a refused recording retains nothing, so with no earlier scan the tool is not run and with one the older scan is read with its timestamp and ruleset shown; split AC-30 into both cases. | spec/functional/FR-142-advisory-join-onto-soup-records.md:145-149, 190 |
| FND-003 | medium | Resolution is per record: a finding is resolved when a current approved fact under the record's single `disposition_clause` was attested at or after the newest scan reporting it. Applying that rule 'unchanged' to policy findings means one attestation, made by a person reviewing an advisory, also resolves every `licence-denied`, `licence-unknown` and `package-banned` finding attached to the record by a scan at or before it, though the fact names none of them. The ticket asks for a person-owned disposition of the licence finding; this gives a disposition of the record. Fix: either state explicitly that a record's disposition covers every advisory and policy finding attached at attestation (and say so in the report row), or key policy-finding resolution on a fact that names the finding's rule id; add an AC with an advisory attestation and a later-unchanged ban finding. | spec/functional/FR-142-advisory-join-onto-soup-records.md:100-105, 153 |
| FND-004 | medium | FR-150-AC-8 reads an SPDX document through the same reader and FR-041 exposes 'SPDX: the declared licence field', but FR-150's table classifies by CycloneDX shape (id, name, expression). In SPDX the declared licence field is always an expression string, and `NOASSERTION` and `NONE` are special values meaning no data. As written `NOASSERTION` is either an id not in allow (`licence-not-allowed`) or an expression (`licence-unknown`), and an SPDX `MIT` is an id or an expression depending on the reader. FR-041-AC-10 covers CycloneDX only. Fix: state that `NOASSERTION` and `NONE` are no licence declared (`licence-unknown`), that an SPDX value that is a single identifier is classed as an id, and add the SPDX cases to FR-041-AC-10 and FR-150-AC-2. | spec/functional/FR-150-licence-and-ban-policy-findings.md:65-75, 116; spec/functional/FR-041-sbom-inventory-evidence.md:93-94, 111 |
| FND-005 | low | `--policy` is a new flag on `quoin evidence record`, but nothing says what happens when it is given with `--adapter sbom`, `grype` or `cargo-deny`. Silently ignoring it lets a person believe a policy was applied. FR-041-AC-9 already sets the precedent of refusing `--discharges` on an adapter that cannot use it. Fix: refuse `--policy` on any adapter other than licence-policy, naming the adapter, and add an AC. | spec/functional/FR-150-licence-and-ban-policy-findings.md:87-89 |
| FND-006 | low | The ruleset states 'the policy's allow, deny and ban entry counts'. Two different policies with the same counts are indistinguishable in the scan state, so a `no policy findings in the scan read` row cannot be traced to the policy that produced it. Fix: record the policy's entries themselves (or its path) as the ruleset; no digest is needed. | spec/functional/FR-150-licence-and-ban-policy-findings.md:80-81 |
