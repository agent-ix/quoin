# Gap analysis — PLAN-007 semantic module contract

## Summary

Audited PLAN-007 (issue `agent-ix/quoin#293`, branch
`spec/293-semantic-module-contract`): TASK-039..TASK-044, the 51 matrix rows
TC-1336..TC-1386, the 51 AC/CON ids under US-020, FR-070..FR-075 and NFR-017,
the six `tests/semantic-*.test.ts` files, the fixtures under
`tests/fixtures/semantic-module/`, and the implementation under `src/semantic/`,
`src/commands/semantic/sweep.ts`, `src/plugins.ts`, `src/catalog.ts`,
`src/write.ts` and the two refresh scripts. Every row and every AC/CON id has a
`// Trace:` tag above an `it(` and the 41 tagged tests pass, but `quire coverage`
binds nothing from `tests/semantic-mapping.test.ts` (its brace scanner trips on a
regex), so 22 rows and 16 criteria are engine-unbacked; the FR-074 promotion
guard does not validate the sweep report against its schema; and 14 rows carry
fixture-level evidence only, two of them with wrong loci.

## Verdict

**FAIL** — two `high` findings (FND-210 engine-unbacked rows, FND-211 a SHALL
clause not implemented despite a green test) and TASK-044 (the review gate
itself) still `todo`. The mediums are real but non-blocking on their own.
